02 3D PRINTING

PERSONAL DESIGN PROJECT

Custom 3D-Printed Medical Brace

Designed a lightweight wrist and forearm brace using 3D-scanned geometry and CAD for a personalized fit. Developed an open lattice structure that reduced material usage by 50% while maintaining structural support, then manufactured and iterated FDM prototypes. I personally had two goals in mind for this project. Needed to be custom to the wearer and designed for manufactering. I wanted a turn around of 24 hours from scan to brace which we achieved

3D Scanning CAD FDM
